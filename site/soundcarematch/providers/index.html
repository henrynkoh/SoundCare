Can your team open the proof?

This check is for adult family home and assisted living teams in Thurston, Lewis, and Mason counties. It is an educational practice tool. It is not a DSHS inspection, and a finished check does not predict a citation.

Answers stay in this browser. Nothing is sent.

1. Can you open the current license and the posted notices without hunting through drawers?
2. Can you show the background check, training, and staff file for each person who needs one?
3. Can staff show the medication record and say what they do if a dose does not match?
4. Can you walk an incident from protecting the resident through the report, the calls, the notes, and the follow-up?
5. Has the team practiced an inspection question by opening the real record, not only talking about it?
6. For each known gap, is there a person, an action, a date, the proof, and a date to check that it lasted?
