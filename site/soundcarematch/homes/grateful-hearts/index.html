SoundCareMatch is not a DSHS, ALTSA, or hospital service. Open beds are the SoundCare WA snapshot from November 25, 2026. Confirm the license, the opening, the services, and the price with the home and with the DSHS facility search. Under RCW 18.330, consultation is free to seniors and families. A care home may pay SoundCare a referral fee after a private-pay move-in. No fee is charged for a placement paid in whole or in part by Medicaid.
