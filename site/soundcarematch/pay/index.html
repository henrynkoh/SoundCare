Paying for care

The four paths below are the ones South Sound families actually mix. Confirm every benefit before you sign a home agreement.

Private pay

You or your family pay the home. Snapshot rates on this site run from $5,000 to $7,000 a month. Ask what supplies, transport, and cable are extra.

Medicaid

Apple Health long-term services go through Home and Community Services. A home may take Medicaid, private pay, or both. SoundCare does not charge a family, and it does not take a fee for a placement paid by Medicaid.

Medicare

Medicare can cover a qualifying skilled nursing or rehab stay. Ongoing help with bathing, meals, and a room in an adult family home needs another way to pay.

WA Cares

Washington's long-term care fund can help eligible workers. Confirm the current benefit and whether a home can use it at wacaresfund.wa.gov before you count on it.

Official starting points: Medicare.gov, WA Cares, and Lewis-Mason-Thurston Area Agency on Aging.
