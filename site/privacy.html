Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

SoundCare AI collects preliminary health care inquiry details, hospital discharge summaries, Activities of Daily Living (ADLs), and contact information voluntarily submitted by seniors, family representatives, and hospital discharge planners.

2. How Information is Processed with Autonomous AI

Information is processed solely to perform multi-constraint clinical matching with licensed Adult Family Homes in Thurston, Lewis, and Mason counties, Washington. We deploy Anthropic’s Claude API under strict enterprise Zero Data Retention (ZDR) and Business Associate Agreement (BAA) standards. Clinical data is never used to train generalized foundation models.

3. Information Sharing & RCW 18.330 Compliance

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data. Information is shared only with prospective licensed Adult Family Homes with explicit client consent to facilitate care tours, intake assessments, and placement agreements.
