# Terms of Service & Statutory Disclosures
Last updated: November 2026
## 1. Nature of Services (RCW 18.330 Mandatory Notice)
SoundCare LLC (doing business as SoundCare AI) operates as an Elder and Vulnerable Adult Referral Agency under Chapter 18.330 of the Revised Code of Washington (RCW). We provide senior care housing referral, placement advisory, and matching services connecting clients with licensed Adult Family Homes (AFHs) and assisted living facilities.
## 2. Fee Structure & No Cost to Families
In accordance with Washington State law, our advisory and matching services are completely free of charge to seniors and their families. Placement fees are paid solely by the receiving Adult Family Home upon successful admission and execution of a resident admission agreement. Under RCW 18.330.070, no fee is charged or collected for any referral paid in whole or in part by Medicaid or public assistance.
## 3. Independent Decision & Client Choice
SoundCare AI is an advisory and placement facilitation agency. We do not provide direct healthcare, nursing, or medical services. The final choice of Adult Family Home, care plan, and admission agreement is made solely by the senior and their designated legal power of attorney/family.
